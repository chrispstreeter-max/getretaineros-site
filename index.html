Quotes and agreements

Quote it. They sign it. You're booked.

Build a quote from your own price list and send it with a plain-English project agreement. Or hand over the iPad and let the client sign with a finger or Apple Pencil.

  • The deposit invoice is ready the moment they sign
  • Signed quotes lock, so the scope can't quietly change
  • Your logo on every quote, agreement and invoice
Projects and timelines

Every project. Every milestone. One look.

Each project gets milestones for its kind of work: website, Shopify, branding, app, SEO. Clients sign off each stage, and change rounds past the agreement get billed in one tap.

  • Client sign-off on milestones, recorded on the agreement
  • Extra rounds of changes flagged and invoiced
  • Links, files, to-dos and time in the same place
Invoices and tax

Get paid. Know what you owe.

Deposit, balance, hourly and retainer invoices with the right GST, HST, PST or QST for where each client is. Overdue ones get a friendly nudge from your Outbox.

  • Quarterly sales tax summary, ready for your return
  • Expenses and profit for every project
  • Monthly care plans and renewals invoice themselves
