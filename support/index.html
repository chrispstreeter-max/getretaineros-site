Use it on your iPhone, iPad and computer

On the computer, open Settings › Data and move your data to iCloud Drive › RetainerOS. On iPhone and iPad it opens from there by itself.

Backups

A copy of your data is kept each day for 14 days in the backups folder next to it. Settings › Data can also export a backup file.

Email

On iPhone and iPad, emails open in the Mail app for you to send. On a computer, connect your mailbox in Settings › Email to send from your own address and turn new inquiries into leads.
