Privacy policy

Short version: we collect nothing. Your work stays on your devices.

Last updated 18 November 2026. RetainerOS is made by Christopher Streeter, CHRISPS Design, Cambridge, Ontario.

What we collect

Nothing. RetainerOS has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. We never receive your clients, projects, invoices, files or email.

Where your data is kept

Everything you enter is saved in one data file on your device. If iCloud Drive is on, it is saved in your own iCloud Drive, in a folder named RetainerOS, so your other devices can open it. Files you add to projects are kept in the same folder. On a computer you choose the folder.

Email

On iPhone and iPad, emails are written by the app and sent by you from the Mail app. On a computer you can connect your own mailbox; its password is encrypted with that computer's keychain and is only used to talk to your own mail server.

Passcode

You can set a passcode that encrypts the data file. If you forget it, the data cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

Children

RetainerOS is a business app and is not directed at children.
