DownbeatOS

Privacy policy

Short version: we collect nothing. Your work stays on your devices.

Last updated 19 November 2026. DownbeatOS is made by Christopher Streeter, CHRISPS Design, Cambridge, Ontario.

What we collect

Nothing. DownbeatOS has no servers, accounts, analytics, advertising, AI or tracking. We never receive your clients, gigs, proposals, invoices, music lists, files or email.

Where your data is kept

Everything you enter is saved in one data file on your device. If iCloud Drive is on, it is saved in your own iCloud Drive, in a folder named DownbeatOS, so your other devices can open it. On a computer you choose the folder.

Email

You can connect your own mailbox (Gmail, iCloud, Zoho and others). The app password is kept in that device's Keychain, never leaves it, and is only used to talk to your own mail server directly. We never see your email. Without a connected mailbox, emails open in your Mail app for you to send.

Your clients

Planning packs, package pages and website enquiry forms made with DownbeatOS send nothing to us. Your clients reply by email from their own mail app, to you. You can export everything kept about a client to answer a privacy request.

Passcode

You can set a passcode that encrypts the data file. If you forget it, the data cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.

Children

DownbeatOS is a business app and is not directed at children.
