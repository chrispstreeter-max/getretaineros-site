Help with DownbeatOS on iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Questions, problems or ideas? Email hello@getretaineros.com or call 226-507-6573. We answer within two business days.

Try it first

On the welcome screen, tap "Explore with sample data" to see a fully set-up business with gigs, a band, a ceremony and invoices. Start fresh any time from Settings › Data.

Use it on your iPhone, iPad and computer

On the computer, open Settings › Data and move your data to iCloud Drive › DownbeatOS. On iPhone and iPad, tap Reload on the welcome screen once it has synced.

Backups

A copy of your data is kept each day for 14 days in the backups folder next to it. Settings › Data can also export a backup file.

Email

Connect your mailbox in Settings › Email to send from your own address and turn enquiries into gigs. Without one, emails open in the Mail app for you to send.
